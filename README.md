# Md. Rasel Rana

**Manager (Technical), Bangladesh Telecommunications Company Limited (BTCL)** · Electrical & Electronic Engineer · Dhaka, Bangladesh

I work where telecommunications infrastructure, electrical engineering and software meet. At BTCL I lead the field operations that deliver telephone and broadband services, work on GPON access networks, and contribute to internal technology projects. Since 2021 I have also built full-stack web applications.
